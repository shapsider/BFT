01 / The idea

Low confidence has a local structure.

In biomedical text, contiguous runs of low-confidence tokens often contain rare entities and mechanistic steps. Treating every uncertain token alike can suppress the knowledge we want the model to learn.

BFT compares each token’s confidence with its surrounding context and gives more weight to sequences containing difficult regions. The two signals allocate learning effort within and across sequences.

02 / Method

One window. Two scales.

BFT uses a centered local window of valid target tokens, with a default size of g = 256. Prompt and padding positions do not contribute to the loss. Confidence-derived weights use stop-gradient.

Within a sequence

ρb,t = clip(cb,t / (Cb,tloc + ε), 0, 1)

Compare token confidence with the local mean. A difficult token retains more signal when its entire context is difficult.

Across sequences

sb = sg(1 − mint Cb,tloc)

Use the weakest local region to assign a bounded sample weight. Sequences with difficult regions receive greater relative emphasis.

Combined objective
LBFT = (1/B) ∑b sb [∑t mb,t sg(ρb,t) ℓb,t] / [∑t mb,t + ε]

03 / Evidence

Biomedical reasoning and RL compatibility.

We independently fine-tune DeepSeek-R1-Distill backbones at 14B, 32B, and 70B using a shared optimization recipe. The medical and biological mixture contains 51,056 filtered samples, with 48,503 in the supervised training split.

04 / Beyond generation

Better profiles support biological representations.

BFT-70B generates biomedical entity profiles. Encoding those profiles with text-embedding-ada-002 yields gene representations, which also support expression-weighted cell embeddings.

For multimodal integration, BFT reaches 0.57 overall scIB, above Harmony and BBKNN but below paired-supervision scMODAL (0.65). Improvements are task-specific and do not establish a general factuality or deployment guarantee.
